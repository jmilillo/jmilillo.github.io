Tip Tips

Some totals just look better.

Tip Tips helps you find a restaurant tip that lands on a satisfyingly repeating total, like $45.45, $101.01, or $9.99. Enter your bill, choose your preferred base tip percentage, and let Tip Tips do the charmingly unnecessary math. Set a default starting percentage, then nudge the tip up or down until the total feels just right, comparing your pick against the base in three playful visualization styles.

Free on the App Store, supports U.S. dollars, and collects no personal data.
