Three Spell

Built for the smallest speller in the house.

Three Spell turns the family fridge into a spelling game. Kids drag magnet letters to build three letter words, match words to pictures, piece together word puzzles, and earn coins to unlock new words for their very own magnet fridge. Settings grow with your speller, from letter hints for beginners to full words for confident readers.

Coming soon to the App Store, made for ages 4–8, and collects no personal data.

A Little History

This one goes back further than the rest. I wrote the first version in Objective-C, on an iPhone 3, as a spelling app for my daughter. It's been sitting in a drawer ever since, and I've slowly been rebuilding and improving it whenever I get the itch.
