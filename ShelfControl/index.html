Shelf Control

Never backtrack down an aisle again.

Grocery lists don't match how stores are laid out, so you end up crisscrossing the same aisles twice. Shelf Control fixes that. Add your stores, map out the aisles, and place each item where you actually find it. From then on, your list sorts itself in aisle order automatically, so you walk in, work the route, and walk out.

$1.99 on the App Store. No data collected.
